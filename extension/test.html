What happens when you click into a field

The same content script runs here and inside the extension on every site. Each step reads the page; none of them rewrites its markup.

  1. DetectA focusin listener reads the real target through shadow DOM and walks up to the editable root: a textarea, a text or search input, or the top contenteditable.
  2. ReadInputs give their value. Rich editors are walked node by node into plain text, with a map from every character back to its text node.
  3. CheckThe rules engine runs locally in a few milliseconds after typing pauses. Claude is called only when you press Deep check or Rewrite.
  4. MeasureRange.getClientRects() gives the boxes for each flagged span. Textareas can’t be measured directly, so an invisible mirror div copies their styles and scroll.
  5. DrawUnderlines, the button, cards and the panel live in one closed shadow root on <html>, clipped to the field and any scrolling parent. Scrolling only moves the layer.
  6. ApplyAccepting selects the span and runs insertText, so the site’s editor sees a normal edit and Ctrl+Z still works.
